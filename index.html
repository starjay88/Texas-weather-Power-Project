ERCOT Grid & Weather Monitor

Real-time correlation analysis between Texas surface temperature and power demand.

* All times are displayed in Texas Local Time (Central Time).

Data Dictionary

Column Name Description Data Type Unit
Time Timestamp of the recorded data Datetime UTC / CT
Temperature Surface air temperature in Texas Float Fahrenheit (°F)
Electricity Demand Total ERCOT power grid demand Integer Megawatt-hours (MWh)
